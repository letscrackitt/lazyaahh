Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information The Lazy Corner collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or submit personal details. Our servers and hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

Local storage

The do-nothing timer saves your longest time in your own browser. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Cookies and advertising

We may use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Third-party services

We load fonts from Google Fonts. These services have their own privacy policies and may receive your IP address when fonts load.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Questions about this policy? Use the contact page.
