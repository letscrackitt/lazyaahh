Why doing nothing is good for you

The Lazy Corner · Rest

Most of us feel guilty when we are not busy. But rest is part of how people stay healthy and think clearly, not a reward for finishing everything.

Your attention needs breaks

Focus is limited. After long stretches of work, mistakes rise and everything feels harder. A few quiet minutes helps you return with a clearer head.

Ideas often show up when you stop pushing

Many people notice solutions arrive in the shower, on a walk or while staring out of a window. Letting your mind wander gives it space to connect things.

Constant stress wears you down

Being always on, with no downtime, is linked with feeling tired and irritable. Regular rest is one of the simplest ways to lower that load.

How to do nothing properly

  • Put your phone out of reach for ten minutes.
  • Sit by a window, lie down or just breathe slowly.
  • Do not turn it into a task. There is nothing to achieve.

Try it with the do-nothing timer on the home page and see how long you can last.
