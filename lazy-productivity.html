Getting more done while doing less

The Lazy Corner · Productivity

Being lazy and being productive are less opposed than they sound. Many "lazy" habits are really about spending effort only where it counts.

1. Pick one thing for the day

Write down the one task that would make the day a success. Do that first, and treat everything else as a bonus.

2. Do the two-minute jobs now

If something takes two minutes, such as a reply or a form, do it immediately. It costs less than remembering it all day.

3. Make hard things start small

Tell yourself you will work on it for five minutes. Starting is the hard part, and many people keep going once they have begun.

4. Rest on purpose

Plan breaks instead of waiting until you are exhausted. A short walk, a snack or a nap often saves more time than it costs.

5. Say no to extra work

Every yes uses up energy. It is fine to decline things that do not matter to you.
