About The Lazy Corner

A small website for people who want permission to rest.

The Lazy Corner started as a simple idea: a place online where doing nothing is the goal. It has a do-nothing timer, a nap mode and an excuse generator, plus short guides on rest and gentle productivity.

It is written and maintained by one person who also works as a teacher and yoga instructor, and who believes rest and calm are worth taking seriously. The guides are general information, not medical advice.
